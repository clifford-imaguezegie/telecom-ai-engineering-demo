# TEAD AI — CV and Professional Portfolio Evidence Brief

*Experimental applied AI engineering project. These are measured development outcomes, not commercial production deployments.*

## CV-ready project description (compact)

**TEAD AI — Agentic Telecom Network Investigation & RCA Platform | Independent Applied AI Engineering Project**

Designed and evaluated an evidence-grounded multi-agent investigation prototype for synthetic 5G RAN accessibility incidents using Python, LangGraph, ML-assisted localisation, domain-specific telemetry tools and knowledge-base RAG. Demonstrated nine recorded investigation scenarios (eight incidents and one healthy control), with SP01 localisation in **15.0–24.3 seconds** and complete incident investigations in **2m 48.2s–4m 32.9s**. Developed deterministic evidence-engineering methods and inference-cost controls, including an experimental SP08 data-representation reduction of approximately **98.7% by byte size**. Preserved explainable RCA conclusions, uncertainty, source provenance and post-run evaluation, while keeping production-performance claims separate from synthetic benchmarks.

## Three achievement bullets (shorter CV version)

- **Architected an experimental agentic 5G network RCA platform** integrating ML-assisted fault localisation, LangGraph-orchestrated specialist investigations, telemetry tools, RAG-supported Supervisor synthesis and traceable engineering evidence.
- **Benchmarked nine synthetic-network investigation runs**, achieving recorded SP01 execution of **15.0–24.3s** and end-to-end incident analysis of **2m48s–4m33s**; separated measured trace latency from demo playback and production SLAs.
- **Optimised LLM evidence preparation and reliability**, including a separate historical SP08 experiment that mechanically reduced an approximately 17.29 MB input representation to 227 KB (**~98.7% fewer bytes**); addressed large prompts, duplicate reasoning passes, retry behaviour and inference-cost governance without claiming independently verified cost savings.
